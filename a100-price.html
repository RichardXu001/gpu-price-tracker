A100 rental price

The A100 is the previous-generation Ampere datacenter card that still carries a large share of production inference workloads. It comes in 40GB and 80GB variants, and rates differ noticeably between them.

How the A100 sits in the market

  • Versus consumer cards. The 80GB variant's tracked median ($1.75) is roughly four times the RTX 4090 median ($0.42, Coloprice Oct 2026) — but you get ECC memory, NVLink on SXM boards and 80GB of HBM-class bandwidth instead of 24GB of GDDR6X.
  • Versus the H100. The H100's mainstream single-GPU band starts about a dollar higher ($2–$7 vs the A100's sub-$2 typical rates), and its reserved pricing rose through 2026 while A100 reserved pricing stayed under $1.60 in the Oct 2026 reading.
  • Variant matters. The 40GB PCIe card rents well below the 80GB SXM card — $1.19 vs $1.89 in the Apr 2026 scan — so filter listings by exact variant before comparing.

Reserved vs on-demand

At the Oct 2026 reading, one-year reserved pricing ($1.59) sat only about 9% under the tracked on-demand median ($1.75). That is a much thinner commitment discount than the consumer tier offers, where reserved listings were quoted at roughly 36% below the on-demand median in the same reading (Novita 12-month rate vs Coloprice median). Treat the comparison as indicative: the two figures come from one tracker's cross-card table and one provider's published rate.
