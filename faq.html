GPU rental pricing FAQ

How much does it cost to rent an RTX 4090 per day?

At the tracked on-demand median of $0.42/hr (Coloprice, Oct 2026), a full 24-hour day costs about $10, and a 24/7 month about $307. Interruptible listings change the picture: tracked spot rates of $0.16–$0.24/hr put a day at roughly $4–$6 (GPUFinder, Oct 2026). Providers increasingly quote daily rates on reserved listings; the tracked 12-month 4090 reserved rate equates to about $6.50/day ($0.27/hr, Novita via Coloprice, Oct 2026).

Why do two trackers show different medians for the same card?

Trackers crawl different provider sets, deduplicate differently, and refresh on different schedules. The H100 "from" rate, for instance, was quoted as $1.68, $1.99 and $2.55 by three aggregators on the same day in October 2026. This site's rule: never mix two trackers in one table, and always carry the source name with the number.

Should I compare medians or floors?

Floors tell you what's technically available; medians tell you what a typical listing costs. A floor listing is often an interruptible, spec-light machine (small system RAM, slow disk), so it is not an apples-to-apples substitute for the median listing. When budgeting, use the median; when experimenting with fault-tolerant batch jobs, the floor can be enough.

Is a cheaper consumer card always the better deal?

For throughput-per-dollar on single-GPU batch work, consumer cards usually win — the 4090's tracked median is about a quarter of the A100 80GB median. Datacenter cards justify their premium with ECC memory, more VRAM (80GB vs 24GB), NVLink on SXM boards and professional availability. Match the card to the workload's memory requirement first, then compare hourly rates within that class.

What else in a listing affects real cost?

  • vCPU RAM and disk. Under-provisioned machines force you to rent a bigger GPU class than the model needs.
  • Egress bandwidth. Matters if you move data or model weights on and off the box frequently.
  • Interruption policy. Pausable-resumable interruptible listings and hard-reclaim spot are priced similarly but behave very differently.
  • Billing granularity. Some services bill per second, some round up to the hour — for short inference jobs this can matter more than the headline hourly rate.

How current are these numbers?

Every figure on this site carries a capture date, and the latest review pass was October 2026. Marketplace prices move daily; treat anything here as a planning reference, then confirm current rates on the provider's own page or a live marketplace listing before committing to a budget.
