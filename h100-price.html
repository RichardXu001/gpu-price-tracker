H100 rental price

The H100 is the current-generation datacenter card most in demand for LLM inference and training. Unlike consumer cards, it is offered almost exclusively by professional clouds, and pricing is often quoted per-GPU within 8-GPU nodes.

Reserved-rate trend

One-year reserved H100 pricing bottomed near $1.70/hr in October 2025 and rose close to 40% through 2026 (source: GetDeploying index cited by Akash, retrieved Sep 2026). The direction matters for planning: the on-demand median softened over the same period, so the gap between committing for a year and paying hourly narrowed.

Enterprise tier beyond the H100

For completeness, tracked rates one rung up the stack (source: gputracker.dev, Apr 2026): H200 from $5.25/hr (CoreWeave), B200 from $7.50/hr, GB200 from $12.00/hr. These SKUs are thinly listed, so treat them as list prices rather than market medians.

Aggregator snapshots differ — a caution

Price aggregators quote different "from" rates for the H100 because they sample different sets: $1.68/hr (Inference Hub), $1.99/hr (gpurentalprices, Oct 2026) and $2.55/hr (Rank.ai) were all live quotes in October 2026. When a rate matters to your build, check the underlying provider's own price page rather than trusting any single aggregator — including this one.
