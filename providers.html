Two market structures, two price logics

Peer-to-peer marketplaces list individual hosts' machines, so prices are set by competition between hosts and the floor tracks electricity and hardware amortization. Professional clouds operate their own datacenters, price higher, and sell availability, networking and support. Both are legitimate choices: batch jobs tend to fit marketplaces, latency-sensitive production services tend to fit professional clouds. A quick way to feel the difference is to open a marketplace such as SpareHive and compare a single card type across hosts — the spread between listings on the same card is the marketplace premium/discount made visible.
